Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the Free Website Health Check?

I review publicly visible indicators of your WordPress website including uptime response, SSL setup, fundamental security headers, page load performance, and core maintenance risks. You receive a concise summary of priority issues after the review.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Managed WordPress Care plans operate on a month-to-month basis. You can pause or cancel service at any time with prior notice before the next billing cycle.

Can we handle billing through Fiverr or Upwork?

Yes. Depending on how you discovered my service, we can set up contracts on Fiverr or Upwork where the platform handles secure payment processing.

What if my site is already hacked or completely broken?

Major malware recovery and site restorations are billed separately as dedicated scope projects prior to enrolling in regular maintenance care.
